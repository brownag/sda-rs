## Notes
- Task scope is documentation only: may edit `planning/reports/duckdb-parser-policy.md` and this TODO; do not touch implementation, tests, Cargo files, existing security policy, routes/configuration, or ADRs.
- Feasibility is bounded: DuckDB dialect parsing and generation worked for sampled constructs, but exhaustive AST child coverage and stable source-to-AST correspondence have not been established.
- Parser accepts multiple statements; generation normalizes comments/quoting. Parsing or successful generation alone is not authorization.
- Existing evidence supports tested SELECT and request-local temp-table workflow; direct DuckDB probes show settings, in-memory ATTACH, persistent in-memory DDL, and compound SQL can succeed absent the executor classifier.
- Unknown functions and external-I/O sources are denied by plan decision; engine capability settings are defense-in-depth, not authorization.
- No implementation authorization is implied. If AST classification cannot be made unambiguous, record the exact decision point and stop for orchestrator/user direction.
- AST completeness remains unresolved: public DFS delegates to a crate-private generated child walker, and the evidence does not prove exhaustive syntax-child coverage or protection against newly added variants. The policy report records this as a blocker for implementation pending a verified completeness/upgrade-guard mechanism or approved scope change.
