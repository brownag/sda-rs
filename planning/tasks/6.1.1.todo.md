## Notes
- Only `docs/spatial-sql-contract.md` may be created; do not edit source, tests, planning files other than this micro-TODO, or external repositories.
- `rewrite_node` handles `geometry::STGeomFromText(wkt, srid)`, `.STGeomFromText(wkt, srid)`, and `.STIntersects(one_geometry_arg)`; the `mukey` TVF expands only for one literal string WKT in supported FROM/IN-subquery shapes.
- The current `mukey` expansion transforms source geometry to metadata SRID (or the documented override fallback 4326), but its WKT input is constructed as SRID 4326; avoid implying generalized CRS harmonization.
- AOI and mukey contract tests demonstrate GeoPackage BLOB conversion with `GeomFromGPB`, real SpatiaLite execution, exact positive results, and a structured unavailable error without the extension. They do not establish general NULL/invalid-geometry semantics.
- ADRs 0001 and 0003 establish SQLite/GeoPackage scope, optional SpatiaLite, metadata-based geometry resolution, and the specific mukey override exception. Plan 5/G32 completion and configured baseline are recorded in `planning/STATE_5.md`.
