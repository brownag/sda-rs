## Discovered
- The prior approved `ST_Read` probe used pinned DuckDB `=1.20000.2`, two synthetic GeoPackages, the authorized Spatial artifact, and direct `Connection` calls. After `SET allowed_paths = ['.../allowed.gpkg']`, both allowed and outside `ST_Read` succeeded (`allowed_read=OK count=1`; `outside_read=OK count=1`). `Config::with("allowed_paths", ...)` failed. This disproves the attempted DuckDB path allowlist approach; it does not test or verify the newly directed private Rust-side `rusqlite` loader.
